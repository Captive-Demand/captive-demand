03: The call

What the call is actually for

We're not going to pitch you. We're going to ask:

  • 01 Where you manage your calendar and bookings today — Airbnb only, Vrbo, a booking system?
  • 02 Whether you have a website, and if so, what's wrong with it
  • 03 How many units you have and what makes them different
  • 04 What a guest goes through today if they want to book direct
  • 05 What you'd want the site to do that it doesn't do now

That's it. Fifteen minutes, maybe twenty if you're chatty. We use it to design something that fits your property instead of a generic template.
